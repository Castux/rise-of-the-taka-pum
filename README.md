# Rise of the Taka-Pum

Lead your army of Taka-Pum to victory using the Heavenly Drums of Awesome!

A rhythm game in which you use drum patterns to give orders to your units. Its name, its look and its concept obviously take inspiration from Patapon, the great PSP game I have actually never played.

- A complete and fairly long in-game tutorial, with a funny back-story. Sort of.
- 15 drum patterns, from basic commands to mystical miracles.

Made in 48 hours for **Mini Ludum Dare #16** (February 2010).

It runs on [evöL](https://github.com/Castux/evol), my own SDL-based Lua engine of the time. It was imported from Launchpad (Bazaar) in 2026, with its full history.

The original builds (2008–2010) are in the [releases](../../releases).
